# Brand decisions

Kept, rejected (binding) and open questions, one block per round.

## Round 1 (2026-11-05)

- Kept: logo direction B Pad bot (game controller with a face and an antenna). Next: 2-3 variants with an educational element.
- Kept: type T2, Fredoka 600 for display and wordmark, Figtree for UI and parent text.
- References from the owner: controller + mortarboard, many colours, thick dark outline, flat fills. Asked for freshness and style.
- No brand PDFs: brand lives in the repo (brandbook/ assets) and in README.md. Assets feed the promo video, then the portal style.
- Open: palette (round 1 P1-P4 and round 2 Q1-Q4), mascot style, sticker vs flat.
