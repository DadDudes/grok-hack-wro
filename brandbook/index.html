01

Brand

1.1The idea

Why EduBotGames looks the way it does.

A parent sets what to learn. The kid says what game to play, and a team of AI agents builds it.

The teal is the school chalkboard. The logo is a game controller in a graduation cap: playing and learning in one face.

Parent picks the topic
Kid picks the game
1.2Principles

Four rules behind every decision in this book.

01

Teach, never cheat

Robo gives hints and asks questions. He never gives away the answer.

02

The kid is the designer

The game looks like the kid's idea, not like ours. We frame it and step back.

03

Calm for parents, bright for kids

Teal and ink build trust. Sun, pink and tangerine are the play colors.

04

Stickers, not gloss

Flat fills, a thick ink line and a hard shadow. No gradients, no blur.

1.3Voice

Talk to kids like a fun older friend. Talk to parents in plain numbers.

  • Short and warmOne idea per line. Kids read it in two seconds.
  • Hint, never tellAsk a question that points the way.
  • Cheer the tryCelebrate in the game's own theme.
  • Numbers for parentsBefore and after, then one next step.

5.3Motion

Every agent step is a pipeline event. Movement stays small and short.

Idle

Bob of 4 px, 2.4 s, ease-in-out. The timeline chip stays white.

animation: bob 2.4s infinite alternate
Working

Glow ring in the agent color, the prop loops: bulb flickers, pencil writes, brush paints, lens sweeps.

glow 1.2s · prop loop 0.4-1s
Handoff

The task card flies to the next agent. The next chip lights up.

500 ms ease-out
Done

Pop to 112% and back, mint check, cheer pose. Reduced motion: color and badge only.

300 ms
